About Asiyah Women's Center

Asiyah Women's Center is the first emergency shelter in New York City specifically for Muslim women and children facing domestic violence, homelessness, or other crises.

They provide a safe, culturally-sensitive environment along with counseling, legal advocacy, food, and housing resources. Their goal is to empower Muslim women with dignity and support.
